# theWorld
Building the future of games. This is a framework to build from. Just the first steps towards a new world goverened fairly where any player can begin with a true digital experience that is unique in every way. To the point even the creators lose control over the experience and go along for the ride themselves. 

Goals and Thoughts::
Enable an ecosystem that will create the future of digital intereactions in a closed loop enviroment via testnet intially similar to no mans sky.

Enable World Masters to work with the 7 Ai God forms to make changes to the story around them. Crafting the ability to obtain real world experience that transaltes directly into a true sandbox experience. 

This world is not limited by science and fantasy but enabled to allow for magic users to interact directly with cyber astetics and magic is the science of the technology to a degree. Ones spells are only limited by imagination and the resource cost that could be the very life of the caster. 

The game will be a single life play and then the ability to play a side character that is only alive for a limited time before fading away. This enables more free play not limited by the users buildout.


This is both a thought experiment to give myself the excuse to put my hands to this world. Though I do consider it to be a lost cause to ever reach the public mind. It enables me to push towards the things I consider impossible. Everytime I reach for this Impossible task I come back with more information. More experience. I urge that if you are reading this that you are apart of something more than yourself. That you are here for the exact same reason I came to this garden of thoughts.
